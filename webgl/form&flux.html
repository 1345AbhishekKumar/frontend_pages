Process

Four movements

6–12 weeks
End to end
01

Listen & Frame

Two weeks of conversations, audits, and sketchbooks. We map the terrain before we build on it. No decks without questions.

Discovery · 2 wks
02

Diverge & Commit

Three distinct directions. We argue, we prototype, we kill two of them. The survivor is the one that makes our stomach flip.

Concept · 2 wks
03

Craft & Break

Pixel by pixel, shader by shader. We build, then we break it on purpose — on old laptops, on phones, at 3AM — to make it unbreakable.

Build · 4–6 wks
04

Launch & Tend

Ship is the beginning, not the end. We watch analytics, fix what we missed, and keep the thing alive long after launch week.

Ship · ongoing
