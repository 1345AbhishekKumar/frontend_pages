N° 05 — Ethos

Nothing extraneous.

Reduction is not absence. It is the discipline of removing everything that does not serve the object, the foot, or the gesture of walking. The Forma carries no logos, no embellishments, no decorative stitching — only the parts that bear load, flex, and endure.

What remains is leather, thread, and the architecture of a last refined over four years of prototyping. A single hide per pair. A single silhouette. A single intention. We make one sneaker, because we could not make this one better by making another.
