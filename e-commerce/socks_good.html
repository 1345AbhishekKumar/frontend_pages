03 — Materials & Craft

The stitch
is the story.

Drag to magnifyfig. 03
240×
i.

Yarn Provenance

Long-staple Inner Mongolian cashmere from a single cooperative in Alashan. Fibres average 14.5 microns — finer than human hair, spun into a 2/48 nm two-ply.

ii.

Knitting Technique

Knitted on a vintage Lonati single-cylinder at 168 needles, 84 courses per inch. Slow gauge, low tension — a dense, dry hand with memory.

iii.

Hand-Linked Toe

Each toe closed stitch-by-stitch on a hand-linking machine, not seamed. The seam sits flat, invisible against skin and shoe.

iv.

Finishing

Boarded in steam, washed in soft mountain water, dried flat. No softeners, no chemical finishes. The sock arrives as it left the loom.
