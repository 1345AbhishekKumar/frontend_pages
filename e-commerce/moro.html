05 / Formation

800 Hours,
6 Months,
1 Jacket.

01 Phase 01120h
Hide Selection Shinki Hikaku · Kobe

One hide in twenty passes. The master runs his palm across the grain, feeling for density, memory, and the slight resistance that says this leather will outlive him. Rejected hides return to the tannery.

02 Phase 02480h
Hand-Stitching Saddle · 8 spi · Waxed linen

Each seam punched with an irons-set, then drawn by hand with waxed linen thread. Two needles pass through the same hole from opposite directions, locking the stitch. A machine cannot feel tension. The hand can.

03 Phase 03200h
Burnishing Iron-dyed · 3 coats · Hand-rubbed

The edge is dyed with iron oxide, sealed with beeswax, and rubbed with a bone slicker until the fibers compress into a glass-smooth surface. Three coats. Three days of drying between each. No shortcuts.

IMMOR
TAL

— Pull, lock, repeat.
480 hours of formation live in this gesture. The thread is drawn taut enough to compress the leather, never tight enough to cut it.
