Materials & Construction

Drag to magnify
the leather.

01.

Full-Grain Calf, Tuscan

Tanned over twenty-eight days in chestnut pits. Traceable to a single tannery in San Miniato, operating since 1898. Each hide bears a unique pore signature.

02.

Vegetable-Tanned Goat Lining

Un-dyed, breathing against the foot. The leather patinas with wear — recording the wearer, softening where the foot flexes, deepening where the heel settles.

03.

Hand-Welted Construction

Seventy-two hours on the last. The leather welt is stitched directly to the upper and insole by hand. The sole is then stitched through the welt — never glued, fully resoleable for decades.

04.

Waxed Cotton Cord

Eight stitches per inch, hand-set with a boar-bristle needle. The thread is waxed — not bonded — so each stitch can be replaced independently over the life of the shoe.

Engineered for the human foot,
not the rack.

Nothing
extraneous.

We make one sneaker. We have only ever made one sneaker.

Every panel exists because the foot demands it. Every stitch is structural. There is no ornament. The leather is allowed to age — to record the wearer, the weather, the walk home. We do not refresh silhouettes seasonally. We refine the same one, perpetually, in the open.

Reduction is not absence. It is the result of refusing to add.

We studied fourteen hundred sneaker silhouettes before drawing the first line of Meridian. Most were noise. We removed the noise. What remained is what the foot has always asked for — a soft, structured, breathing envelope. Nothing more. Nothing that cannot be repaired.
