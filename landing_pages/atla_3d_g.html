/ 02 — ACCURACY

Know the accuracy of your LLM app

Stop relying on vibes. Atla turns subjective quality into rigorous, reproducible signals your team can act on.

01

Automate labeling

Replace expensive human raters with Atla-1, our frontier evaluation model. It labels responses on the same scale your domain experts use — at 1000× the speed and a fraction of the cost.

  • 92% agreement with expert raters on legal tasks
  • Custom rubrics in plain English
  • No prompt engineering required
Read the technical report
02

Gain control over every output

Trace every score back to its source. Drill into failure modes by user, prompt template, retrieval chunk or model version. Quality stops being a black box.

  • Slice metrics by any metadata field
  • Regression detection across deploys
  • Native OpenTelemetry export
03

Filter out the worst outputs

Gate every generation behind an Atla score. Low-confidence responses are held back, retried, or routed to a fallback model — your users only ever see your best work.

  • Score-based routing & retries
  • Sub-100ms p95 latency
  • Deploy as a sidecar or proxy
04

Install in seconds

One pip install. Drop Atla into your existing stack — LangChain, LlamaIndex, Vercel AI SDK, or raw OpenAI calls. No infrastructure to manage, no models to host.

$ pip install atla-ai
>>> from atla import Atla
>>> atla.score(response, criteria="factual accuracy")
