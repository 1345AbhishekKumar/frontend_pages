07 — FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Everything you need to know about applying, building, and scaling with Nexus Labs.

Still curious?
Talk to our team
What stage should my startup be at to apply?
We accept founders from idea-stage through early mainnet. The most successful applicants have a working thesis, a technical co-founder, and the conviction to ship in 12 weeks. We do not require traction or prior funding.
What equity do you take?
Standard terms are 7% equity for $500K initial capital, with optional follow-on of up to $5M at Series A. Token side is negotiated separately with a 3-5% token allocation to the studio.
Do I need to be in Singapore?
No. The program is hybrid. Founders are expected to be in Singapore for two intensive weeks (kickoff and Demo Day), with the remainder remote. We provide co-working access globally through our partner network.
Which blockchains do you support?
Ethereum, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, Avalanche, BNB Chain and Sui. We also support Bitcoin L2s and app-chain deployments through our RaaS partners.
When is the next cohort?
Cohort 06 kicks off in March 2026. Applications open in November and close January 15. Rolling admissions for exceptional teams are available between cohorts.
