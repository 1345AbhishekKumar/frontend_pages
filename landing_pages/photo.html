A Study in Material Memory

We collect what the world forgets to hold.

Pressed petals, torn wool, exposed silver. Each artifact carries the weight of a gesture — someone's hand, someone's hour, someone's refusal to let the moment vanish without witness. The archive is not a museum. It is a keeping.
