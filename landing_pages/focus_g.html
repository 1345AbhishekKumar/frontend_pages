Chapter 04 — Portrait

A study in stillness

Shot over three sessions in a quiet studio on the edge of the city, this series resists performance. The subject does not pose; they remain. What is left is the architecture of attention — the small, involuntary gestures of the eye that reveal more than any spoken word.

Printed on archival platinum paper, each edition is numbered, signed, and accompanied by a handwritten note from the artist describing the exact minute the shutter closed.
