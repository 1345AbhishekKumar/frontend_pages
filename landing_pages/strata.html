Field notes — straight answers 04 / 09

Asked in every boardroom.

01How is this different from a BI dashboard?+

A dashboard waits for you to look. Strata looks for you — it forms a view, defends it with citations, and only escalates when something actually deviates from the model of your business it maintains. You get a briefing, not a blank canvas.

02Will the model hallucinate numbers?+

It can't cite what it can't query. Every figure in a briefing is traced back to a warehouse row through the governance ledger — if the SQL receipt doesn't exist, the claim doesn't ship. In two years of production briefings: zero ungrounded numbers.

03Where does our data live?+

Wherever you say. EU or US residency, deployed inside your VPC or fully on-prem for regulated estates. Inference runs against your warehouse — Strata keeps zero retention of row-level data once the briefing is sealed.

04How long until the first briefing?+

Connect a source, and a provisional briefing lands in under 90 seconds. The sentinels need roughly 72 hours to learn seasonality; after that, alerts carry confidence intervals instead of guesses.

05How is it priced?+

Per signal, per seat, per month — flat. No per-query metering, no "contact sales" fog on the pricing page. If Strata doesn't surface a signal you act on in a month, that month is free.
