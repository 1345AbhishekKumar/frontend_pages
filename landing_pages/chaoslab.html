Manifesto
Reading time · 42 sec

We believe the best work is made when tools disappear and instinct takes over. No templates. No guardrails. Just a canvas that breathes with you.

Most design tools treat creativity as a feature to be managed — dropdowns, panels, presets. Chaoslab inverts that. Every interaction is tuned to the rhythm of your hand, the velocity of your idea.

We built this for the 1% who open Figma and feel trapped by the rectangle. For those who design like painters and ship like engineers.

Questions, Answered

The questions our community types into the ⌘K bar most often. Still stuck? Talk to us →
chaoslab — faq.sh — live
12 answers · updated today
Q.01 Is there a free plan? +
Yes. The Free plan is free forever, not a 14-day tease. You get unlimited canvases, three of the four Rooms, PNG/SVG export, and full access to the community gallery. We only charge when you need collaboration, motion, or the Palette AI.
Q.02 Can I import from Figma? +
One-click import. Figma, Sketch, Adobe XD, Illustrator, SVG, PDF — all supported. Your layers, components, and styles come through cleanly. Most studios migrate a full design system in under an hour.
Q.03 Does it work offline? +
Fully. Chaoslab is a local-first app that happens to sync. Work on a plane, in a tunnel, on a mountaintop. Your changes merge automatically the moment you reconnect — no conflicts, no lost work.
Q.04 Is my data private? +
Your work is yours. We never use your designs to train our models unless you explicitly opt in. SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, end-to-end encrypted in transit and at rest. Atelier plan customers can run Chaoslab in their own VPC.
Q.05 What about AI training on my work? +
Opt-in only. Atelier plan customers can actually train private models on their own brand — so the AI suggests on-brand palettes, type pairings, and layouts that sound like your studio. Your IP stays your IP.
Q.06 What platforms is Chaoslab on? +
Web (any modern browser), native apps for macOS, Windows, iPad, and Vision Pro. All share the same engine, same files, same keystrokes. Start on your Mac, finish on the train, present on the iPad.
Q.07 Can my whole studio switch at once? +
We've onboarded studios of 3 to studios of 340. Atelier plan includes a dedicated onboarding lead, SSO/SCIM, and a 30-day parallel-run period where both Figma and Chaoslab stay in sync. Zero downtime migrations.
