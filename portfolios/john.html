03 / About

I started coding out of curiosity — and never quite stopped.

Building small browser games and landing pages turned into developing complete products that balance design and engineering. The thing that hooked me then is the same thing that hooks me now: the moment when something you imagined becomes something you can click.

My stack includes TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node, and PostgreSQL — but I love exploring new tools that make the web feel a little more considered.

Outside of coding, I write, contribute to open source, and teach others what I've learned. I also walk a French Bulldog named Pixel.
